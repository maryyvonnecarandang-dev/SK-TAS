<h1 align="center"> SK-TAS </h1>
<h2 align="center"> Sanggunian ng Kabataan Transparency and Accountability System </h2>

- This system will help everyone to know the projects that happened and will happen in the future. The public will also see the receipt of every project and know how much sk officers spend on it. A system that promotes transparency to everyone.

---
## Problem Being Addressed
- Many governance issues occurring in local communities across the Philippines involve SK officers and a lack of accessible budget transparency, leaving the public unaware of where youth funds actually go. Without a clear reporting channel, citizens cannot verify project costs or track upcoming initiatives. This system addresses the issue by allowing everyone to see completed and upcoming projects, inspect official receipts for every expenditure, and verify how much SK officers spend on each program

---

## Project Objectives
- **Promote Financial Transparency:** Provide the public with read-only access to approved budgets, itemized expenses, timestamps, and official receipts for every SK project.
- **Enhance Project Tracking:** Allow the community to monitor project lifecycles from initial planning to completion.
- **Encourage Youth & Citizen Participation:** Enable residents and youth to submit project proposals, vote on community priorities, and raise questions or feedback on unclear statements.
- **Streamline SK Council Operations:** Equip SK Chairpersons, Treasurers, and Committee Heads with structured, role-based tools for budget management, reporting, and analytics.

---

## Target Users
- **Katipunan ng Kabataan (Youth Members):** Youth aged 15–30 who want to monitor SK funds, propose youth-oriented projects, vote on initiatives, and engage with their local council.
- **Barangay Residents / General Public:** Community members seeking visibility into local governance, project accomplishments, and public spending.
- **SK Chairperson:** Leads project approvals, oversees council operations, and publishes official reports and projects to the public portal.
- **SK Treasurer:** Manages budget allocations, records cash inflows and outflows, uploads official receipts, and generates financial summaries.
- **SK Councilors / Committee Heads:** Update project implementation progress and respond to community inquiries within their assigned committees.
- **System Administrator:** Manages user accounts, role assignments, and term-based officer transitions.

---

## Main Features
- **Youth & Officer Profiling:** Maintains structured records of registered users and elected or appointed SK officers, tracked by term to accommodate changing administrations.
- **Role-Based Control:** Enables administrators and authorized officers to operate flexibly according to strictly defined system permissions.
- **Project Planning & Monitoring:** Moves projects through five distinct stages—Planning, Budget Approval, Implementation, Monitoring, and Completed—allowing everyone to track progress.
- **Budget Allocation & Expenses Management:** Records every incoming and outgoing budget transaction along with its statement of purpose, timestamp, and receipt documentation.
- **Public Transparency:** Allows citizens to view approved budgets, project statuses, official receipts, accomplishments, and financial reports through a read-only public portal.
- **Community Suggestions & Voting:** Enables youth and residents to propose community projects and vote on suggested programs, helping SK officials prioritize initiatives based on community needs.
- **Budget Analytics Dashboard:** Provides visual reports showing budget distribution by category, expenditure trends, and remaining funds to support sound financial planning.
- **Comment and Feedback:** Gives youth and the public a space to contribute project ideas and post comments or questions regarding unclear financial or project statements.
